How is pricing calculated?

Plans are priced per student per year. Tell us your student count and the modules you need, and we will send a quote.

Is setup included?

Yes. Setup, data migration from your spreadsheets, staff training and ongoing support are included in every plan.

Can we start with one module and add more later?

Yes. Many institutes start with Essentials and add CBT exams and analysis later. Your data carries over.

How do Group plans work?

Group plans are quoted for your branches and total student count, with a multi-branch dashboard and a dedicated account manager.
