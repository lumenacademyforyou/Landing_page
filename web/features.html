AI-Powered Assessment

CBT Exams & Question Engine

Timed computer-based tests conducted on lab PCs, tablets, or phones with automated question shuffling and auto-grading.

  • AI blueprint generator aligned with board syllabus
  • Dynamic question & option shuffling per student
  • Instant automated grading for objective questions
  • Immediate post-exam feedback and score release
Diagnostic AI

AI Performance & Weak-Topic Diagnosis

Deep topic-level diagnostic insights flagging learning gaps by student, section, and class, recommending practice questions automatically.

  • Weak chapters and sub-topics highlighted instantly
  • Longitudinal performance trends across terms
  • Automated remedial question sets generated for students
  • Marks seamlessly feed into digital report cards
Proctoring & Security

Secure Exam Mode & Lockdown

Full-screen kiosk window lockdown that records test-window exits and enforces rigorous anti-cheating controls.

  • Kiosk full-screen browser lockdown
  • Audit trail of every window exit and tab change
  • Role-based exam creator, invigilator, and controller roles
  • Local offline-sync resilience during connectivity drops
Campus Operations

Admissions & Paperless Enrolment

Online admission enquiries, document uploads, fee verification, and one-click enrolment into the master student record.

  • Branded online enquiry and application portals
  • Digital document verification and approval workflows
  • Automatic roll number, section, and student ID generation
  • Instant circulars, notices, and transfer certificate issuance
Campus Operations

Smart Attendance & Biometrics

Teachers mark attendance in seconds from mobile devices, or sync directly with RFID turnstiles and biometric scanners.

  • Instant mobile marking for class teachers
  • Automated morning absence alerts to parents via WhatsApp/SMS
  • Staff attendance directly connected to monthly payroll
  • Comprehensive daily, monthly, and board-mandated audit reports
Campus Operations

Timetable & Faculty Substitution

Clash-free automated schedule generation for classes, labs, and faculty workloads with real-time teacher substitution alerts.

  • Automated conflict detection for rooms, labs, and faculty
  • One-tap teacher substitution when staff are on leave
  • Personalized student and teacher weekly schedules on mobile
  • Classroom and lab capacity optimization
Finance Ledger

Fees, Concessions & Receipts

Multi-installment fee heads, scholarship concessions, UPI and card payment links, automated digital receipts and due reminders.

  • Customizable fee slabs (tuition, transport, lab, activities)

  • Instant automated digital receipts with institute seal
  • Automated WhatsApp fee reminders before due dates

  • Monthly payroll summaries and bank transfer sheets for management

  • Transport fee mapping by distance and route
