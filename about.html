What is Scored?

Your smart partner for running an institute

Scored is a web platform that brings institute administration and computer-based exams together. Attendance, payroll, admissions, CBT exams and analysis share one student record, so your team stops copying data between spreadsheets.

  • One login for office staff, teachers, the principal and parents
  • Your spreadsheet data migrated for you
  • Works on lab PCs, laptops and phones
